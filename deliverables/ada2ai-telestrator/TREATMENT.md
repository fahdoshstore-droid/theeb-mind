# Treatment — "Footage → Intelligence" (Sports Telestrator grammar, as a website component)

Adapted from the Lemo-Opuscar workflow (DIRECTOR.md §4), shortened because the output is a page component, not a film.
Reference used for **grammar only**: `styles/telestrator/STYLE.md`. No footage, branding, fonts, colours, code or sound was copied.

## Three candidate structures
1. **Chronological build-up** — scene → freeze → rings → arrows → finding → board. One piece of action taken one layer deeper at a time.
2. **One moment from three angles** — needs real multi-angle footage; ADA2AI has none. Rejected.
3. **"What went wrong" defensive breakdown ending on the space map** — needs a real incident and real positions. Rejected.

**Chosen: 1.** It is the only one that stays honest with a single illustrative still, and it maps directly to the brief
(MATCH FOOTAGE → FREEZE FRAME → PLAYER TRACKING → TACTICAL ANNOTATION → ANALYSIS INSIGHT → BOARD).

## Logline
Watch the moment, stop it, ring the people, draw what matters, say one thing about each colour, then flatten it into a board.

## Benchmark — learn / don't take
- **Learn:** watch-then-freeze order; one finding per freeze with one label; marker lines drawn along their length with a dark outline and glow; perspective rings under the feet; a hatched patch for space; a corner bug present in every frame; the pitch flattening into a board; one colour = one identity.
- **Don't take:** any broadcaster's logo/colours/idents; the demo's match, story, shots or timings; the beIN SPORTS CC BY footage (third-party broadcast with logo, unclear rights chain for a commercial product).

## Beat sheet (autoplay, ≈14 s, then rests on the board)
| t | Step | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| 0–2.6 s | 1 Scene | `REPLAY` bug; slow push-in on the frame |
| 2.6–4.5 | 2 Freeze | white flash, punch 1.08→1.045, desaturate, heavier vignette; bug → `ANALYSIS` |
| 4.5–7.1 | 3 Tracking | six rings draw clockwise, ID chips drop in (A = ours, B = opponents) |
| 7.1–10.7 | 4 Drawing | space patch fades in; pass arc, run and pressure arrow draw along their length, arrowheads pop at 90 % |
| 10.7–14.5 | 5 Insight | three black label cards (Arabic + spaced English line, role-coloured bar) + the reading panel |
| 14.5 → | 6 Board | picture tips back and fades, navy board rises with the same tokens and lines; rests here with ↻ |

## What the sequence is NOT (honesty contract)
There is no real footage and no tracker output. The frame is an AI-generated still; rings, IDs, paths, patch, labels, observations and the board are hand-drawn illustration with a permanent «توضيحي — ليس تحليلاً آلياً» badge and an explicit note. No speeds, distances, times, counts or identities appear anywhere.
